2026 Margaret Annan Memorial Prize in Poetry

Awarded to Sophia Zeglis, judged by Richard Siken

A lyric poem concerns itself with how it says more than what it says. A lyric poem is song, is sound and rhythm and detail. The poems in this submission are propelled by associative leaps and emotional logic. They are thick with images and voiced in a personal grammar. Something grand and mythic is happening here, though it is presented casually. The images are striking; the insights unsettling. William Carlos Williams said, famously, No ideas but in things. These poems follow that dictate. They ground their thinking in image; in the lush, tangible world.

—Richard Siken, judge
